"Taksh" comes from "Takshan" — to carve, create and craft. ARCHITECT & FOUNDER, STUDIO TAKSH

Ar. Richa Chhabra is registered with the Council of Architecture (COA) and holds a Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) from Vivekanand Education Society's College of Architecture, Mumbai.

Her experience spans residential projects, institutional projects, interior and landscape design, with her journey extending from academic years to professional practice.
